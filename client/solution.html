A block of mass m is placed on a wedge of mass M. The wedge is placed on a smooth horizontal surface. If the coefficient of friction between the block and the wedge is μ, calculate the minimum horizontal acceleration a required to keep the block stationary relative to the wedge.

Solution Breakdown

menu_book

Official Solution

1

Analyze Forces

Consider the Free Body Diagram (FBD) of the block in the frame of the wedge. Since the wedge is accelerating with a, a pseudo force ma acts on the block towards the left.

2

Equilibrium Conditions

For the block to be stationary relative to the wedge, forces parallel to the incline must balance.

mg sin(θ) = ma cos(θ) + μN
3

Solve for Acceleration

Substituting normal force N and simplifying for minimum acceleration.

Final Answer

a = g ( sinθ - μcosθ ) / ( cosθ + μsinθ )
